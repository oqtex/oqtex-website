OQmega Team Wins First Place and 150,000 EGP at Cairo AI Hackathon

In December 2025, the OQmega Team won first place at Cairo AI Hackathon and received a 150,000 EGP prize, marking a major achievement for OQTEX's applied AI and research capabilities.

The winning project was Neural Ocular Network (NON), named OQgen, an AI system that can detect Retinopathy Of Prematurity (ROP) in newborns in less than 2 seconds.

The hackathon was organized by Athar Accelerator and sponsored by Plan International Egypt, creating a strong platform for showcasing high-impact Egyptian AI innovation.
